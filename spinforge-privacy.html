SpinForge privacy policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

SpinForge: Ball Battle Arena ("SpinForge") is made by Ben Thomas ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles and why.

What the game stores on your device

Your currencies (Orbs and Gems), unlocked balls, cosmetics, Battle Pass and quest progress, and settings are stored on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Online play (Unity Gaming Services)

SpinForge uses Unity Gaming Services, provided by Unity Technologies, for its online features:

  • Anonymous sign-in. The first time you play, the game creates an anonymous player ID. There is no email, password or social-media login, and we do not ask for your real name.
  • Player name. Your in-game name is built from pre-approved word lists (for example "NobleWizard"). You cannot type free text, so it never contains personal information.
  • Ranked and "ghost" battles. When you play Ranked, the game uploads a snapshot of your fighter: your player name, the ball you used, your hidden skill rating, and play-style statistics (such as how often you dash, shield or use your Ultimate). Other players may battle this snapshot and see your player name.
  • Technical data. When the game connects to these services, Unity receives standard technical information such as your IP address, device model and operating system, which it uses to run and secure the service.

Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Purchases

Any real-money purchases (such as Gem packs, the Starter Pack, the Battle Pass or King Kitty) are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details; we only receive confirmation of what was bought so we can deliver it. Forge Crates cannot be bought and are earned only through play. Drop rates are shown in the store.

Advertising

SpinForge does not show ads and does not use advertising identifiers.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera.
  • There is no chat and no user-written text.

Children

SpinForge is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us and we will delete the associated data.

Keeping and deleting your data

Online data (your anonymous player ID, player name and ranked snapshot) is kept while your account is active. To ask for it to be deleted, email us with your player name (shown on the main menu, for example NobleWizard#13175) and we will delete it within 30 days. Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests can be sent to the developer contact email shown on the app's store listing.
