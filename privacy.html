Shelf Cat privacy policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Shelf Cat is made by Ben Thomas ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles.

What the game itself collects

Nothing. Shelf Cat has no account system, no sign-in and no servers of its own. Your score, treats, unlocked skins and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Shelf Cat shows ads through Google AdMob. When an ad is loaded, Google's advertising SDK may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device type and operating system, approximate location derived from the IP address, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this to serve and measure ads and, where you have consented, to personalise them.

  • In regions that require consent (for example the EEA and UK) you will see a consent form on first launch and can change your choice later from the device's ad settings.
  • On iOS, the app asks for permission to track before any personalised ad is shown. Declining does not change how the game plays.
  • Rewarded ads are only shown when you choose to watch one.

Google's own policy explains their handling in detail: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

Purchases (RevenueCat, Apple, Google)

The one-time "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. To recognise the purchase across reinstalls, we use RevenueCat, which stores an anonymous app-specific identifier and your purchase history for this app. RevenueCat's policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Children

Shelf Cat is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads are configured according to the applicable store's family policies.

Your choices

  • Turn off personalised ads in your device settings (iOS: Settings, Privacy, Tracking; Android: Settings, Google, Ads).
  • Buy Remove Ads to stop interstitial ads entirely.
  • Uninstall the app to delete all locally stored game data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact email shown on the app's store listing.
